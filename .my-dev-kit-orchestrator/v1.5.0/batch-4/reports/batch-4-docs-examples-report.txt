Explicit overclaim prevention notes
===============================================================================

Every new doc passage was checked against the task's overclaim list before
being written:
- No claim of runtime UI proof, browser execution, or database
  introspection anywhere in the new classification text - every mention of
  "static"/"conservative" pairs classification with the same qualifiers
  already used for data-model/frontend-reachability elsewhere in these same
  docs, for consistency.
- No claim of LLM semantic similarity.
- v1.6 planner packets/context capsules, v1.7 benchmarks, and plugin
  architecture are explicitly named as NOT part of v1.5.0 in
  docs/PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md's new "does not claim" bullet and
  docs/ROADMAP.md's rewritten "Future work for this area" subsection - not
  just silently omitted.
- No claim that classification makes an automatic or authoritative "safe to
  edit" decision - docs/PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md's new bullet and the
  classification-contract's own edit-guidance framing (advisory, evidence-
  backed, always paired with an uncertainty tier) are stated explicitly.
- The "Context report improvements" roadmap subsection (reachable-today,
  read-only-vs-editable, safe-to-modify-first aggregation) was NOT invented
  as implemented - it is explicitly labeled as not built in v1.5.0.
