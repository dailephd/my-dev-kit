Semantic artifacts discovered (existing, v1.1-v1.4):
- data-model.json, data-model-graph.json, model-view-lineage (referenced by
  IndexSemanticArtifacts.modelViewLineage), frontend-semantic.json,
  frontend-reachability.json - all registered via IndexSemanticArtifacts in
  manifest.json and via IndexAnalyzerStatus entries keyed by IndexAnalyzerId
  ('syntax' | 'call-graph' | 'data-model' | 'model-view-lineage' |
  'frontend-semantic' | 'frontend-reachability' | extensible string).
- Compact per-node/per-symbol metadata already exists today as
  `semanticRoles?: SemanticRole[]` and `artifactRefs?: SemanticArtifactRef[]` on
  both CodeGraphNode (code-graph.json) and GraphSymbolRecord (symbol-index.json).
- SemanticRole already carries: role (SemanticRoleName), subtype, confidence
  (SemanticRoleConfidence: 'explicit' | 'inferred-static' | 'partial' | 'unknown'),
  source (SemanticRoleSource, extensible string), artifactRefs, evidenceRefs
  (SemanticEvidenceRef: filePath, symbolId, line, endLine, source, analyzer),
  warnings (SemanticRoleWarning: kind + message; kind is one of
  'ambiguous-evidence' | 'missing-artifact-ref' | 'missing-source-ref' |
  'partial-classification' | 'unsupported-pattern' | 'unknown').
- Critical finding: SemanticRoleName ALREADY includes most of v1.5's target
  categories: canonical-type, database-model, artifact-type, projection-type,
  view-model, ui-only-state, persistence-adapter, route-handler,
  client-component, server-component, test-fixture (plus data-entity, data-field,
  schema-model, react-component, test-block, browser-storage-payload,
  storage-key, rendered-field, unknown). This is v1.1/v1.2/v1.4 vocabulary for
  symbol/type-level roles derived from the data-model and frontend analyzers.
- Enrichment pattern: applySemanticRolesToCodeGraph(codeGraph, metadataBySymbolId)
  maps over graph nodes, matches by node.id via a Map, and spreads
  { semanticRoles, artifactRefs } onto matching 'symbol'-kind nodes only
  (file-kind and other node kinds are left untouched). Confirmed by direct source
  retrieval that applySemanticRolesToSymbolIndex.ts performs the equivalent
  enrichment on GraphSymbolRecord entries in symbol-index.json, keyed by
  buildSymbolId(file.path, symbol.name) instead of node.id.
